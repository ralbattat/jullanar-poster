Jullanar Poster – Terms of Service

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Jullanar Poster ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by the Jullanar music project ("we", "us"). It uploads music videos and short clips that we created ourselves to our own TikTok account, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

1. Who may use the app

The app is used only by the Jullanar project to post to TikTok accounts that we own. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

2. What the app does

When we connect our TikTok account, TikTok gives the app a posting token. The app then uploads our own videos, with the captions we wrote, either into the TikTok inbox of our account (to be finished in the TikTok app) or directly to our profile. The app never posts content that we did not make and never acts on any other account.

3. Content

All music, lyrics, pictures and video clips posted through the app are original works of the Jullanar project. We are responsible for everything the app posts and we follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

4. Changes and termination

We may change or stop the app at any time. The connection to TikTok can be removed at any time from TikTok's app settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services).

5. Contact

Questions about the app: contact the Jullanar project through the TikTok account the app posts to.
