Jullanar Poster – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Jullanar Poster ("the app") is a private tool that the Jullanar music project uses to post its own videos to its own TikTok account through TikTok's official API. This page explains what information the app handles.

What the app receives

When we connect our TikTok account, TikTok sends the app an access token, a refresh token and basic profile information about that account (display name, avatar, the account's open ID, and which posting settings TikTok allows). The app receives nothing about any other TikTok user.

How it is stored and used

The tokens and profile details are stored only on the computer that runs the app, in a local file, and are used solely to upload our videos and captions to our account. They are never sent to any server of ours or to any third party. The app keeps a local log of which videos it posted and when.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data, does not track anyone, uses no advertising or analytics, and never reads or stores TikTok passwords – sign-in happens on TikTok's own pages.

Deleting the data

Disconnecting the app in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) revokes its token immediately. Deleting the app's local folder removes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the Jullanar project through the TikTok account the app posts to.
